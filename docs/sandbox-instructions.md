The LLM sandbox is a way to test your understanding of Python coding
concepts (or coding concepts in general) without writing code. Your prompts
reveal how you think about problems—your conceptual clarity shows through
the words you choose and the details you include.

When you load the LLM sandbox link, your browser (use desktop Chrome) 
will download a small AI.

You will be given a programming task.

Instead of writing Python code, you can only use English to describe 
the task to this small AI. The AI will generate the Python code for you.

After generating the code, you can click "Run" to execute the code
to see if it solves the problem.

You cannot modify the generated code. If you think your prompt is 
inadequate, you will need to modify and refine your prompt. Each revision
shows your thinking process—how you diagnose problems and clarify your ideas.

Your job is to show your thinking through prompts. A strong prompt demonstrates:

- **Precision**: Don't just say "print a number"—say "print an integer" or 
  "print a float." Specific vocabulary shows you understand the distinction.
- **Clarity**: Explain *what* the code should do and *why*, not just the steps.
- **Completeness**: Include edge cases, data types, and constraints you discover.

Because the output of modern AI systems is somewhat random, the same
prompt will produce different code.

If the AI generates incorrect code, there are two possibilities: either your
prompt is inadequate, or the AI is wrong. Analyze your prompt carefully:
Did you explain the logic clearly? Did you miss a key Python concept? 
Are you being precise enough? Refine your prompt and try
again. Your revisions are where the real learning happens.

Once you think your prompt is strong enough, hit the "submit" button. It will
submit the history of your prompts to me, as well as giving you a chance to copy
your own prompt history into commercial AIs like ChatGPT or Gemini, where you
can get some feedback on your thinking process.
